Plain-word glossary
How clear the problem is (Cynefin)
Clear: the answer is obvious, so just do it. Complicated: an expert can work it out, so study it first. Complex: nobody can know ahead, so try it small and see.
Hard choice / big choice / no-brainer
How hard the call is. A no-brainer is cheap and easy to undo. A hard choice has real costs either way.
18-point score
Each move scores 1 to 3 on evidence, money, effort and how easy it is to undo. Evidence and money count double. 18 is the best.
Learning phase
The first days of a Meta ad set, when Meta is still testing who to show it to. Results swing until it has enough sales.
Lift test
An experiment. One group sees the ads and a similar group does not. The gap in sales is what the ads really caused.
Publisher group
One publisher (or Meta itself). It counts once for a question, however often it wrote about it. The Questions page lists them all.
Circles 1 to 4 and insights C to F
The four circles are the owner's sketch: 1 Features now, 2 Problems, 3 Business benefits, 4 How features are used and measured. An insight is where two circles overlap, for example C is circles 1 and 2. The Story page says which circle or insight each card lights up.
Solid, dashed and dotted arrows
Solid = stated in v3, not tested. Dashed = waiting in line: one test per account at a time (sequence). Dotted = our guess (assumed).
Pixel
A small piece of code on a website that tells Meta who visited and who bought.
Advantage+
Meta's automatic setting. Meta's AI picks the audience, places and budget for you.
Retargeting
Showing ads again to people who already visited your shop or looked at a product.
Holdout
A group of people kept from seeing the ads, so you can compare them with the people who did.
Incremental
Extra sales that happened because of the ads. Sales that would have happened anyway do not count.
Lookalike
An audience of new people who are like your best buyers.
Attribution window
How many days after seeing or clicking an ad Meta still gives the ad credit for a sale.
Value bidding
Telling Meta to chase the highest sales value, not just the most sales.
Conversions API (CAPI)
A way for your own server to send sales to Meta, even when the website pixel misses them.
Campaign
The top level in Meta Ads Manager. It sets one goal, such as sales or chats, and holds one or more ad sets.
Ad set
A group of ads inside a campaign. It has its own audience, places to show and budget.
Placement
A spot where an ad can show, such as the Facebook feed, Instagram Reels or Messenger.
Business portfolio
The Meta account where a business keeps its ad accounts, Pages and the people who may use them.
Crossref
A free public list of research papers. We use it to check that each paper we cite is real and its title matches.
DOI
A paper's permanent web address. A DOI link goes straight to the paper's page.
Untools
A free website of thinking methods. Each one is a short list of steps for solving a problem or making a decision.
CPM
What you pay for 1,000 views of an ad (cost per 1,000 impressions). A low CPM means views are cheap.
ThruPlay
A video view that counts when someone watches 15 seconds or to the end, whichever comes first.
Hook rate
The share of people who watch at least the first 3 seconds of a video ad. It shows whether the opening grabs people.
Eisenhower matrix
A way to sort tasks by two questions: how urgent, how important. It says what to do now, schedule, hand off or leave for later.
