    What exactly counts as "viewed" and "bought"?

    Write each rule once, with what it leaves out. Most wrong numbers start with a vague definition.

    Logged-in viewer

    Opened the product page while signed in, in the last 7 days.

    • Any device: phone and laptop are one person
    • Many views still make one row
    • Visitors who never signed in
    • Staff, test accounts and bots (eligibility check; none in this data)
    Example: Quân viewed on a phone and a laptop. That is 1 viewer.
    Bought

    A completed sale of the same product, online or in a store, on or after the first view and by the run date (18 Nov 2026, Vietnam time).

    • Online order with status "completed"
    • Store sale linked by loyalty card or phone
    • Cancelled orders and items left in a cart
    • A different product
    Example: Hà's order was cancelled. Hà has not bought.
    To message

    A logged-in viewer with no "bought" for that product.

    • One row per person and product
    • Rebuilt every morning
    • People who opted out of marketing (eligibility check)
    • Visitors we can't name
    Example: Dũng viewed the sofa and bought a lamp. Dũng is on the list.

    How do we know the web shopper and the store buyer are the same person?

    We link them with exact keys only: the website login, the loyalty card and the phone number. A wrong link costs more than a missed one.

    The one hard decision

    ChosenExact match only

    • Link a store sale to a person only by login, loyalty card or phone.
    • Wrong merges are rare and easy to trace. A key that two people share links no one.

    Cost: a cash sale with no card or phone stays unlinked. Here that is 1 of 4 store sales (Ngọc).

    RejectedBest-guess match

    • A model guesses from device, Wi-Fi, area and time.
    • Finds more matches.

    Why not: one wrong guess drops a real lead, or messages someone about a sofa they never saw. It is hard to audit and risky for privacy.

    Store sales linked to a person

    Identity resolution
    linking records from different systems to one person.
    Exact (deterministic) match
    the same login, card number or phone.
    Best-guess (probabilistic) match
    a model guesses from patterns such as device and place.

      Runs every day proposed

      • 02:00Store sales arrive overnight.
      • 02:30Re-read 2 days of store sales (late uploads).
      • 06:00Rebuild the list (recent days only).
      • 06:15Run the tests; stop if any fail.
      • 07:00Send to the CRM journey.

      If a check fails

      Owner: the analytics engineer on duty; the CRM manager is told.

      1. Hold the 07:00 send.
      2. Find the cause: late till data, a broken join or a key clash.
      3. Fix, rebuild the list and rerun the tests.
      4. Send once. Sends are keyed by person, product and run date, so nobody gets two.
